# I'm Lennard

A fullstack product leader and builder, based in Stockholm. I turn complex problems into products that work — owning the full loop from user research and strategy through architecture, code, and delivery. I care about craft: clean systems, thoughtful interfaces, and shipping things that hold up.

Right now I'm building product at DIB Travel, shaping how companies book and manage business travel. Before that, I ran backend systems, booking flows, and payment infrastructure at [Check24 Flug](https://flug.check24.de/), one of Germany's largest flight comparison platforms. I started my career building insurance products and driving innovation initiatives at [Hypoport](https://www.hypoport.com/).

Outside of work, I build tools that scratch my own itch — focused on productivity, developer tooling, and AI:

<Project slug="indexed"/> makes Jira, Confluence, and local documents searchable for LLMs. It's a privacy-first CLI tool that indexes your existing knowledge bases and serves them over MCP — so AI assistants can actually find what you need.

<Project slug="shards-agent"/> is a fork of Craft's open-source AI agent app, rebuilt around markdown and note-taking. It pairs a Claude-powered agent with a built-in editor — handling context, versioning, and retrieval so you can focus on writing.

<Project slug="obsidian-task-ui"/> brings visual task management to Obsidian. Kanban boards, table views, and drag-and-drop — built on top of the dataview and tasks plugins you're already using.
